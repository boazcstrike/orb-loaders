Three Dots - eight single-element dot loaders

Picked from three-dots by Zongbin (MIT). One element per loader, plain CSS, no script. Colour is currentColor; every length comes from one property, --td-size.

Colour

1 · The eight

All shown at --td-size: 12px. Each card's snippet is the whole markup.

flashing · .td-flashing
Three dots fade up in a left-to-right wave. The calmest of the set.
Pattern: role="status" + label for a page or panel wait.
<div class="td-flashing" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></div>
pulse · .td-pulse
Dots swell and settle in turn. Never shrinks to nothing, so it reads at a glance.
Pattern: role="status" + label; the default general-purpose pick.
<div class="td-pulse" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></div>
elastic · .td-elastic
A vertical stretch rolls across the row, like a wave through rubber.
Pattern: role="status" + label.
<div class="td-elastic" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></div>
typing · .td-typing
Each dot hops and brightens in turn. The chat "someone is typing" idiom.
Pattern: role="status" + a specific label, e.g. "Assistant is replying".
<div class="td-typing" role="status" aria-label="Replying"></div>
falling · .td-falling
Dots drop in, hold, then fall away. Slows in, speeds out, like gravity.
Pattern: role="status" + label.
<div class="td-falling" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></div>
collision · .td-collision
Newton's cradle: outer dots swing out and strike back, one side then the other.
Pattern: role="status" + label. Wider footprint (6 dots).
<div class="td-collision" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></div>
revolution · .td-revolution
Two dots orbit a centre dot at different speeds. Big footprint: shown at 7px.
Pattern: role="status" + label; hero or empty-state use.
<div class="td-revolution" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></div>
windmill · .td-windmill
A triangle of dots turns in eased 120° steps, clicking from one pose to the next.
Pattern: role="status" + label.
<div class="td-windmill" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></div>

2 · One property, every size

--td-size is the dot diameter; the whole loader scales with it, and its margins are its real footprint. 3 px sits inline with body text, 12 px suits a panel.

3 · In context

Inline beside text, inside a button, and as a chat bubble. Decorative loaders sit next to visible words and are hidden from assistive tech.

4 · Dropping it into a page

One file, no build step, no script.

<!-- 1. load the stylesheet -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="three-dots.css">

<!-- 2. one element; colour and size are ordinary CSS -->
<div class="td-pulse" role="status" aria-label="Loading"
     style="color: var(--accent); --td-size: 10px"></div>

<!-- decorative, beside visible text -->
<span class="td-flashing" aria-hidden="true" style="--td-size: 3px"></span> Saving
<div class="td-pulse" role="status" aria-label="Loading" style="--td-size: 10px"></div>
  • Reduced motion: the dots hold their rest pose and the whole loader breathes in opacity, slowly. Turn it on in your OS to see it.
