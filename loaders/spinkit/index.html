SpinKit - eight CSS spinners

A hand-picked, retuned subset of SpinKit by Tobias Ahlin. Pure CSS, no JavaScript, no dependencies. Colour follows currentColor, size follows --sk-size. Variant choice cross-checked against Android-SpinKit by ybq. Both MIT.

56 px

1 · The eight

Chosen for range: a ring, a wave, a pulse, a grid, a fold. Dropped from upstream: plane (a flat flipping square), pulse and circle (near-duplicates of bounce and circle-fade), and wander. Use the size slider to see how each holds up small.

2 · One variant, every size

Dots and bars have a minimum pixel size, so at 16 to 24 px they stay legible instead of dissolving into sub-pixel smudges.

3 · In context

The same markup, three jobs. The button spinner is white because it inherits the button's text colour, with no extra rule.
